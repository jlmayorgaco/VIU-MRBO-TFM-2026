CONCLUSION In this research, we introduced decentralized deep Q-network (DQN) controllers in a multi-robot system (MRS) to solve an object transportation task cooperatively. The controllers learned how to behave and cooperate from scratch. Given the state of the MRS, the DQN controllers can output discretized control signals directly without knowing the dynamics nor planning a path. Two training algorithms were proposed to adapt homogeneous and heterogeneous MRS, respectively. In the future, we would like to continue exploring the potential of MRS’s with more advanced DRL algorithms and settings.
