\noindent{\sffamily\bfseries\color{VIUOrange}\fontsize{18}{22}\selectfont Abstract}\par
\vspace{0.8em}

This Master's Thesis studies coalitions of autonomous mobile robots for transporting heterogeneous loads. An assignment may meet the requested robot count and still fail because of limited capacity, poor contact geometry, insufficient dynamic authority, collision, or stale information. A \emph{physical coalition} passes these checks without conflating static acceptance with transport success.

The main contribution is A0--FULL, a cumulative diagnostic chain evaluated on one Euler--Lagrange plant. A0 is not a formal game: it computes per-robot utilities and applies a global threshold. Subsequent stages add integer quorum closure, effective capacity, contact complementarity, dynamic reserve with HOCBF, and recovery under network degradation; several of these mechanisms are game- or price-based. SP1--SP8 provide modular evidence rather than one end-to-end execution. The canonical thresholds, closures, and replacement step still use global information; the evidence supports audited distributed components, not an end-to-end local implementation.

The corrective protocol was frozen before opening fresh seeds and fixed $n=100$ worlds per family: 400 independent worlds and 2,400 method runs. Its twenty consecutive contrasts used exact McNemar tests, paired bootstrap intervals with frozen analysis seeds, and Holm correction, with no optional stopping.

Under scarcity, integer closure increased success by $0.56$, effective capacity added $0.44$, and wrench closure then reduced success by $0.16$ after removing traction reserve. Paired contact search added $0.63$ under torque; safe mechanics and recovery added $0.22$ and $0.67$ under an obstacle and dropout. FULL achieved $0.89$--$1.00$ success but retained 11\% failures in the most adverse regime. Its selector uses global candidates: this is a recovery proxy, not evidence of fully distributed local closure.

The modular evidence also preserves negative results. SP4 aggregates six scenarios within 18 independent seed-by-fleet blocks; all five preregistered directions survive Holm correction. Conversely, the MAPPO-GNN audit blocks any attribution of success to the learned policy: RAW success is zero and deterministic repair drives the closed result. The theoretical development establishes a practical ISS result under a common Lyapunov matrix and explicit assumptions. The scope is planar simulation; the thesis makes no claim of physical validation, demonstrated functional safety, or universal superiority.
