# EIC review report

## Recommendation

**Minor Revision** for a Master's Thesis; **Major Revision** if submitted unchanged as a journal article. Confidence: 4/5.

## Summary

The thesis addresses a relevant systems problem and now has a legible central contribution: A0--FULL shows how integer closure, capacity, contact geometry, dynamic reserve, HOCBF filtering and replacement interact on one numerical plant. The strongest editorial decision is the refusal to force a winner. The $-0.18$ A2--A3 effect in scarcity and the residual $14\%$ collision rate make the argument more credible. The freeze, width-only extensions and post-hoc claim audit distinguish the work from a collection of attractive simulations.

The remaining issue is emphasis. SP1--SP8 still occupy most of the results chapter and can make the reader mistake the contribution for eight partially mature campaigns. The title promises distributed coordination, whereas the integrated replacement selector is global and several historical closures are also global. The revised abstract and limitations disclose this, but the contribution statement should consistently call FULL a recovery proxy. With that final alignment, the work is defensible and examination-ready.

## Strengths

1. **Clear empirical delta**: Section 6.1 isolates the new knowledge: static wrench closure can reduce dynamic authority.
2. **Negative results retained**: Sections 6.1 and 7 report residual failures and non-significant effects.
3. **Auditable lifecycle**: the protocol froze before test, retained 2,160 unique rows and records the seed-opening event.
4. **Theory bounded by assumptions**: Theorem 5.1 states fixed load, common matrix, no reset and bounded input.

## Weaknesses and actions

1. **Distributed label remains broader than evidence (Major)**. FULL uses a global candidate pool. Keep “distributed” as the architectural research objective, but state in the contribution paragraph and results synthesis that the integrated recovery evidence is not end-to-end decentralized.
2. **Legacy material dilutes novelty (Minor)**. Open Chapter 6 with a one-paragraph hierarchy: A0--FULL is the integrated primary campaign; SP1--SP8 are mechanism studies with unequal maturity.
3. **Novelty statement is diffuse (Minor)**. State the delta in one sentence: the contribution is the executable certificate ladder and the demonstrated non-equivalence between static feasibility and dynamic authority, not a new HOCBF or a universally new ISS theorem.
