2. Re-evaluate saved checkpoints on a fixed reconstruction of the declared validation grid.
3. Audit statistical finiteness and p-value rendering.
4. Retrospectively map failures without changing source rows.
5. Fail closed for lifecycle, stage separation, or rebuild evidence that cannot be demonstrated.

Prohibited: training, confirmatory seed opening, campaign execution, and writes to the source experiment.
